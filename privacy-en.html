2. Data processing in the app

PROTEIN PLUS is an app for tracking your personal protein intake. Foods you create, serving sizes, protein values, daily goals, settings and history entries are stored exclusively on your iPhone. No user accounts are created for these features, and no nutrition data is transmitted to us or to external services.

3. Trial period

To manage the 30-day period of free use, the app may store the start of the trial locally in the iOS Keychain. This information is used solely to manage access.

4. In-app subscriptions

Paid access is provided through Apple's in-app purchase system (StoreKit). Apple handles purchases and subscription management. The app checks subscription status through the interfaces provided by Apple. We do not receive payment details such as credit card numbers through this process. Apple's privacy information applies to processing carried out by Apple.

5. No proprietary tracking services

According to the provider, the app does not use any proprietary analytics, advertising or tracking services.

6. Contacting us

If you contact us by email, we process the information you provide in order to respond to your enquiry. Depending on the nature of the enquiry, the legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) or (f) of the GDPR. The data is deleted once it is no longer needed to handle your enquiry, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

7. Your rights

Subject to the provisions of the GDPR, you have, in particular, rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. As your nutrition data is stored exclusively on your device, we have no access to that data.

8. Changes

This policy will be updated if the app's features or data processing activities change.
